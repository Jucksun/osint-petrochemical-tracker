    Sources. SEC EDGAR (registrants, latest 10-K, Exhibit 21 subsidiary list) and EPA ECHO (facility compliance and penalty records).

    Linking. A CIK identifies an SEC registrant, not a corporate family, and most subsidiaries have no CIK. Facilities are tied to the family by name matching against Exhibit 21:

    • ex21_exact: facility name equals a subsidiary name (strongest).
    • ex21_prefix: facility name starts with a subsidiary name (needs review).
    • token_only: only a distinctive word such as "Dow" matched. Weak; can be unrelated companies or oil wells.

    Penalties are agency-reported and may be alleged, pending, appealed or settled. They are never summed across tiers.

    Map positions are ZIP-code centroids, so they are approximate and not the facility footprint.
